## How to use this bank

Every question here is original and grouped by the concept it tests, so you can pull a set for any session. Each subject has its own tab, and the answers with explanations sit in a separate tab nested under it, so you can share or print the questions on their own.

- **Grid-in** marks an SAT student-produced response: the student types the answer, with no choices.
- **ACT** marks a math topic the ACT tests but the digital SAT rarely or never does.
- English questions are labeled **SAT style** (one short passage, one question) or **ACT style** (an underlined portion with a NO CHANGE option).
- Multiple-choice questions have four choices, matching both tests now that the ACT has dropped its fifth math choice.

Passages and data sets were written for practice. Named researchers, surveys and experiments are fictional, while historical and scientific background is real. None of the questions come from the official free practice tests, so students can still take those fresh.

## Using the answer tabs

Every answer row names the wrong choice students pick most often and explains why it fails. When a student misses a question, check which choice they picked. If it's the tempting one, the row tells you which misconception to reteach. If it's a different choice, the miss was more likely a slip or a rushed read, and the fix is pacing rather than content.

The Science answers also tag each question with one of the ACT's three science reporting categories. If a student's ACT score report shows a weak category, you can pull the matching questions.
