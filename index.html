How it works

Every student starts in a different place, so we start by finding out where you are.

Questions families ask

Don't see yours? Call, text or email me any time.

Where do sessions happen?

At the Glenview Public Library, 1930 Glenview Road. I strongly prefer meeting in person because it's better for focus (and more fun), but online sessions are available on request. If you have a Glenview library card, you can reserve a study room for us.

What happens in the free intro?

We meet for 30 minutes, in person or online. I'll ask about your goals, test dates and what has felt hard so far, we'll try a short lesson together, and I'll suggest a plan. There's no obligation to sign up.

How often should we meet?

One or two sessions a week of one to two hours each works best for most students. It builds momentum without overloading you on top of homework and activities. If a test date is close, we can add a little more for a week or two.

Should I take the SAT or the ACT?

Illinois public high school juniors take the ACT at school in the spring, so most students around here focus on it. Some also try the SAT. If you're not sure, take a free practice test of each and we'll compare your results at the intro.

Do you teach ACT Science?

Yes, as an add-on. Mention it when you book and we'll build it into your sessions.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Let me know at least 24 hours before the session and there's no charge. Things come up, so if it's last-minute, reach out anyway and we'll try to find another time that week.

Do prepaid hours expire? Can I get a refund?

Prepaid hours are good for six months from purchase, and unused hours are refundable. If you stop partway through a test prep pack, the hours you've used are counted at the single-session rate of $70 and the rest comes back to you. For example, if you use 6 hours, you get $80 back.

How does payment work?

Single sessions are paid when you book. Weekly plans are billed at the start of each month, and the test prep pack is paid up front or in two installments.

Do you have a referral reward?

Yes. When a family you refer signs up, you get a free hour of tutoring.
